# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Short-form video ad creators who lean on AI generation tools (Sora/Runway/Kling-style video
generators, image generators) as part of their workflow. They're mid-workflow, moving fast between
tools, not settling in for a marketing read. Two distinct moments: before generating (need a
concrete creative angle, not a blank page) and after generating (need to clean up a rough output
into something postable).

## Product Purpose

Slate is two small, independent companion tools that sit around an AI video-ad generation step:

- **Plan** (`/plan`): turn a product URL or brief into 8 structured ad hook variants, one per fixed
  creative-testing archetype (Bold Claim, POV/Relatable, Problem-First, Curiosity Gap, Controversial
  Take, Before/After, Social Proof, Myth-Bust), each with a ready-to-paste video-generation prompt.
- **Polish** (`/polish`): upload a generated image and sharpen it (2x/4x upscale) into a crisp final
  asset, with a before/after comparison and a direct download.

No login, no database, fully stateless. Success looks like: land, do the one task, leave with a
usable result, in under a minute of actual interaction (excluding model latency).

## Brand Personality

Quiet, disciplined, editorial. Vocabulary is grounded in film/video production (a slate, a shot
list, numbered takes) rather than generic SaaS language. One deliberate visual signature (the
slate-stripe logomark) carries the boldness; everything else — typography, cards, spacing — stays
restrained and consistent. Reads as a considered tool built by people who make video ads, not a
template.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI tool" scaffolding: tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, arrow (→)
  suffixed button text, numbered 01/02/03 section markers used as decoration rather than a real
  sequence, identical rounded cards with the same soft grey shadow.
- Washed-out grey secondary text ("AI slop" body copy) — secondary text should be dark
  navy/ink-adjacent, not pale grey-on-off-white.
- Gradients as decoration, glassmorphism, hero-metric-template blocks.
- Empty-feeling bordered boxes with no real content (icon-only placeholders standing in for actual
  preview content).
- A generic multi-color SaaS palette. The palette is deliberately restrained: off-white bg, ink,
  muted dark navy, one accent blue, one light-blue tint, one hairline border. Small, justified
  exceptions (a semantic error red) are fine; a broad palette is not.

## Design Principles

- One signature element, everything else quiet. The slate-stripe logomark is the one place the
  design gets loud; typography, cards, and spacing stay disciplined and consistent everywhere else.
- Ground vocabulary in the actual subject (film/video production), not generic SaaS/AI-tool
  patterns.
- Show, don't leave empty. A preview, a real example, a real image beats an abstract placeholder
  box every time — empty bordered boxes read as unfinished, not minimal.
- Flat, hairline-bordered surfaces over shadow-heavy floating cards.
- Text must read as intentional, not templated: no pale grey-on-white, no arrow-suffixed CTAs, no
  eyebrow-label reflexes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large/bold text ≥3:1, `prefers-reduced-motion`
respected for any animation. No additional stated requirements yet.
